## Tests expected from the human

1. Open WebMSX above, or use ASCII16 on standard-speed MSX1 at100%,64KiB RAM,
   PSG only. Enable browser sound with a tap/click. F1 starts/resets; arrows
   move/jump, Space/fire performs the original action. Mobile virtual controls
   are available but have not been physically validated here.
2. Compare v20/v21, especially stages with the moving bear. Expect the same
   bear shape/color/position and collisions, with shorter presentation gaps.
   Observe both directions, bank edges and day/night transitions when possible.
3. Jump left/right through all four ice rows, land and immediately jump again.
   Check unchanged character colors, contact response and jump trajectory.
   Report any remaining slideshow effect or missed-looking landing.
4. Turn ice blue, reverse rows, repeat wraparound and complete/enter the igloo.
   Expect no holes, stale tiles, flashing bear, footer corruption or fragments
   waiting at the edges. Dense enemies must retain their count and movement.
5. Send a short recording with sound, version, emulator/MSX model and NTSC/PAL.
   Mark stage/actions where a hitch occurs. Human testing does not block the
   next automated performance investigation while you are away.
