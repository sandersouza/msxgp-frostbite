# v0.0.18 — Tile-aligned playfield diagnostic

This tests the owner's selected visual option 1: the four enemy and four ice
bands use 24-pixel vertical spacing. Artwork/colors/counts and the black left
mask remain. Bailey moves as a complete sprite group so his feet track the new
ice positions. Original simulation, collision rules, controls and sound order
remain unchanged in the bounded automated checks. Anchors move by up to 7 pixels.
This layout is experimental; the previous ROM remains available for rollback.

Stage populations differ; stage means are observed costs, not paired CPU
savings. Original simulation stays near 59.92 steps/s. The live bear window gains
4.08% NTSC and effectively0% PAL. **Stable 30 FPS and original fluidity are not
achieved.** P95/maximum gaps remain roughly66.7/83.4 ms NTSC and59.8/79.8 ms PAL.
Some individual landings regress despite the aggregate improvement: NTSC arcs
5/7/15 and PAL 7/13 lose their grounded image. Physics/contact state is unchanged.

## Checks and limitations

575 host tests; 2,115 source scenes; 1,920 derived density scenes; 54 header cases;
2,115audio intervals with 3,499 source / 2,472 PSG writes in exact order. Full
NTSC/expanded-PAL source CPU/all-RAM, declared pixels/owners and active-page
checks pass. Eighteen generic and four new-helper real IRQ boundaries pass;
AF2 corruption is rejected. Independent rebuild reproduces all three builder
modes and verifies allocations, 512 literal deltas and 539 natural SAT translations.

Final clean-source routes reproduce all five measured buffers. Exact-source
CI passes. The attached workbook preserves 31 historical sheets / 775,979 cells and
adds the whole-route costs and all 26 landings per TV mode. Formulas/export were
audited; five regions were visually checked. Native Excel was not exercised.
Manual listening, all-level, owner-fluidity and physical-hardware acceptance
remain pending. Controlled density and directed IRQ tests are separate from
natural performance measurements.

## Tests requested from the human

1. Load this ROM with ASCII16 in the same MSX1 profile at 100% speed used for v17.
   Test NTSC and PAL separately when possible. Tap F1 to start. Arrows/joystick
   move and jump; Space/fire performs the original action.
2. Alternate v17/v18 from a fresh start. Judge whether the slightly tighter
   vertical spacing is acceptable and whether motion is visibly smoother.
   No color, shape or actor should be missing. The left mask remains black.
3. Jump up/down through all four ice rows. Land and immediately jump again;
   steer left/right while airborne. Watch feet/ice contact and any apparent
   floating, sinking, abrupt vertical step or changed collision behavior.
4. Reverse rows, activate blue ice, build/enter the igloo and cycle the aurora.
   Expect the same colors and progression, with no stale tiles or row seams.
   Play far enough for moving bears and denser enemies if practical.
5. Send a short recording with audio, ROM version, NTSC/PAL profile and emulator
   version or MSX model. Mark any hitch/contact glitch and say whether the new
   spacing is acceptable. We especially need A/B landing and horizontal-motion
   feedback; the measured gain does not promise fully smooth motion.
