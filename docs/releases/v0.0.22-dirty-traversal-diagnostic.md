   PSG only. Tap/click for audio. F1 starts/resets; arrows move/jump, Space/fire
   performs the original action. Mobile virtual controls are available;
   physical mobile-control behavior has not been verified here.
2. Compare v21/v22 with repeated jumps through all four ice rows, steering
   continuously left and right. Land and immediately jump again. Expect the
   same shape/colors/contact response; report hitches or missed-looking landings.
3. Turn rows blue, reverse them, wait for wraparound, complete and enter the
   igloo, then progress to moving bears/dense enemies if practical. Expect no
   stale tiles, holes, flashing bear, footer damage or fragments at the edges.

   expected; controls should remain responsive and sound behavior unchanged.
