   PSG only. Tap/click for sound. F1 starts/resets; arrows move/jump, Space/fire
   performs the original action. Mobile virtual controls have not been tested here.
2. Compare v23/v24 while repeatedly jumping/steering through all four ice rows.
   Watch the moment the temperature units digit changes. Expect unchanged
   digit shape/colors, degree icon, score/lives and no stale fragments or flashes.
3. Immediately jump after landings, turn rows blue/reverse them, approach both
   shore edges, complete/enter the igloo and progress to bear/dense enemies if
   practical. Expect identical contacts, actor counts, controls and sound.
