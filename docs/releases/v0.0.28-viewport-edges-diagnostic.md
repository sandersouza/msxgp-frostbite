1. Load ASCII16 on a100% standard-speed MSX1,64KiB RAM,16KiB VRAM,PSG only,
   or open WebMSX. F1 starts/resets; arrows move/jump; Space/fire acts.
2. Let an enemy or the bear drag the player toward the left. Expect gradual
   clipping at the visible field edge, with no premature disappearance or trail.
   Try this on the shore and ice, facing both ways and after changing direction.

   overflowing fragment of that same enemy at the left before entry. Separate
   copies already moving at the left must remain visible and behave normally.

   Expect original colors/fade/contacts and no newly slow dragged-left frames.
