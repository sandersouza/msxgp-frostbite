This implements the owner's selected option 3. Seven black mask sprites are
replaced by a tile-aligned sea edge at X24, cropping four extra pixels at X20..23,
Y64..175. Header and footer remain unchanged. P0 retains its multicolor artwork,
grid24 contacts and foreground priority. Enemy entry/exit clipping accounts for
the new edge; no waiting object should remain visible outside it. Mechanics,
original source/input/audio clock and actor counts are preserved.

## Human tests

1. Open the WebMSX link above or load the ROM as ASCII16 in an MSX1 profile at
   100% speed. Tap/click the browser screen to enable sound. Use touch controls
   or a keyboard/joystick; virtual/physical F1 starts/resets, arrows move/jump,
   Space/fire performs the original action. Test NTSC/PAL separately if possible.
2. Compare with v18. Watch enemies/fish entering and leaving the left edge,
   including dense rows and wraparound. Expect the narrow four-pixel crop and
   no stationary fragments waiting outside the field.
3. Steer left/right during jumps, land and immediately jump again through all
   ice rows. Check unchanged colors/contact/response and whether fewer poses
   are skipped. P0 remains foreground and may overhang the extra border near
   the extreme left; it should not lose colors or flicker.
4. Activate blue ice, reverse rows, build/enter the igloo and continue to moving
   bears/denser enemies. Check cold starts and later cache reuse for stale tiles.
5. Send a short recording with audio, ROM version, emulator/MSX model and TV
   mode. Mark any edge fragment, flicker, contact mismatch or hitch. Testing
   this build does not need to block the next automated optimization attempt.
