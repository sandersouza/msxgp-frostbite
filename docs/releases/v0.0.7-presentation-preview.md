# v0.0.7-presentation-preview — visual and PSG diagnostic

This prerelease adds the original-derived scene, animated player and objects,
ice floes, score, temperature, reserves, igloo and ordered built-in PSG audio.
It is an early **presentation/audio diagnostic**. It is **too slow for judging
original gameplay rhythm, difficulty or collision timing**. Issue #14 remains
open; this release does not declare the port or real-time integration complete.

## Human tests requested

Please keep the initial display configuration; F3/F4 diagnostic layouts are not
part of this preview's visual qualification. Use normal machine/emulator speed.

1. **Cold start / idle:** wait about 10 seconds. Capture the whole screen. Check
   that Frostbite graphics and the HUD are legible, without old Seaquest artwork,
   missing rows, corrupted tiles or unexplained colors. Report persistent
   corruption separately from brief tearing during a slow update.
2. **Start and movement:** tap **F1** (original Reset/start), then use **arrows or
   joystick** to move/jump. **Space or joystick fire** reverses a contacted blue
   row on a fresh press. Confirm controls respond and the scene continues to
   change. Expect noticeable slowdown; do not evaluate authentic speed yet.
3. **Sound:** listen while landing on ice, reversing a row, collecting a fish
   or losing a life when reachable. Report missing audio, harsh/crackling output,
   an obviously stuck tone or an effect that never ends. A short video with sound
   and the action that triggered it is particularly useful. Timbre is approximate
   and durations can stretch with the renderer's known delays.
4. **Reset / select:** press F1 during an audible effect and confirm a fresh game
   appears with sound returning to its appropriate state, rather than a stuck
   old effect. F2 is original Game Select: zero/one/two/three short presses select
   regular one-player / regular alternating / advanced one-player / advanced
   alternating; press F1 to start. Report any freeze or persistent audio/HUD
   corruption, including the selected variation.
5. **Two controllers, if available:** in an alternating variation, check that
   joystick ports1/2 control their respective active players and continue working
   while sounds play. The keyboard follows the active player. State clearly
   whether this was checked in an emulator or on physical hardware.

Please return the release/version, machine/BIOS/VDP, NTSC or PAL, emulator and
version or physical cartridge/loader, selected variation, exact actions and a
screenshot/video with sound. Appearance, sound and input feedback are useful now;
original rhythm/difficulty and physical compatibility remain separate verdicts.
