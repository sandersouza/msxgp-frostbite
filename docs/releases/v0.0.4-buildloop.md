# v0.0.4-buildloop — native floe/build-loop diagnostic

This prerelease replaces scripted floor contacts with original-clock floe
acquisition. The native game core now drives the build-and-enter loop with
original activation/rearming, fire-edge reversal/costs, temperature and entry
ordering. It is a diagnostic step toward the complete port.

- ROM: `frostbite-0.0.4-buildloop.rom`, 4,194,304 bytes, ASCII16.
- SHA-256: `c298a0311eb263b4e2fdc5ce490d29880ac27ebbc92f53e492f17b7f5747d600`.
- Source: [`81619c0`](https://github.com/sandersouza/msxgp-frostbite/commit/81619c058e04a644564404ec4bd8582ac46cee41).
- Target: standard-speed MSX1, 64 KiB RAM, 16 KiB TMS9918-family VRAM; no expansion,
  turbo, disk or additional sound hardware. This build is silent.

## Current checks and limitations

Clean automated checks pass: 203 host tests; 8,704,000 controlled source cases with 228 literal
player views, 4,497 generic/1,793 specialized CPU cases, 1,368 visible-adapter cases,
86 whole-ROM boundary cases, and the continuous 2,254-state original route.
The actual paced ROM also matches every source CPU/RAM checkpoint in NTSC/PAL,
with 4,139 completed frames checked pixel by pixel. Thirteen stress scenarios
add 3,120 frames; eight deliberate faults are rejected. The tightest measured
PAL stress margin is 232 cycles; natural PAL margin is 1,159 cycles.

The natural route constructs the igloo, enters and initializes the next level.
It loses three reserves and finishes with zero; it is not a deathless route.
No source RAM/contact injections or disabled source hazards are used there.

Presentation is synthetic. Igloo progress is coarse, but the completed door
appears only at original CD0F. Original graphics/HUD/audio are still pending.
Full hazard/fish/bear/shore collision acquisition is issue #12, including
invisible edge contacts. Later-level and complete-game acceptance are pending.
Temperature, score and reserves are not fully shown in this diagnostic HUD;
their numerical transitions are checked automatically. These results do not
claim manual-emulator or physical-hardware acceptance.

## Human tests requested

1. Load the attached ROM with mapper **ASCII16** on an ordinary 64 KiB MSX1
   or equivalent emulator at normal speed. Try NTSC and PAL when available.
   Record the machine/BIOS/VDP, emulator version and mapper.
2. Press F1 to start/reset. Use arrows/joystick to move and jump; Space/joystick fire reverses the
   contacted row on a fresh press. F1 resets, F2 cycles original variations.
   Keyboard controls follow the active player; joysticks use ports 1/2.
3. Land on white floes: they should turn blue. Blue landings should not add
   another block; incomplete all-blue rows should whiten after their cooldown.
   Build the coarse igloo until its door appears, then reach the shore near
   the doorway and press Up. Expect entry, bonus processing and a fresh round.
4. On a grounded blue row, release/repress fire and watch its direction reverse.
   Holding fire should not repeatedly reverse it. Block costs and completed-
   igloo free reversals are covered by the automated numerical checks.
5. F3 changes diagnostic layout and F4 changes width, once per press. Exercise
   both while moving, especially in PAL. Expect stable frames without flicker,
   pauses or a fifth-sprite disappearance. These keys do not change physics.

Send reproducible steps and, if possible, a screenshot/video for unexpected
landing, jumping, reversal, entry or pacing behavior. Include F3/F4 settings and
whether the result came from an emulator or a physical MSX. Silence and the
synthetic visuals are expected at this stage; hazard behavior is incomplete.
