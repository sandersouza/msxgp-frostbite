# v0.0.12 — Player palette and selective header diagnostic

The normal player now has a yellow hat, cream face/legs and gray torso. The
palette change is limited to visible player pixels. Terminal/death animation
keeps its previous colors and four-plane limit. Other graphics are unchanged.

Header updates now rebuild only columns affected by changed glyphs or colors,
retaining their fixed physical tiles and restoring their NAME entries. The
original logic, input, collision, jump and ordered PSG routines are unchanged.

## Measured result and limits

Compared with v0.0.11 on the same 2,252-step input-only route:

| Active gameplay | v0.0.11 | v0.0.12 | Gain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| NTSC pictures/s | 10.3356 | 10.6131 | 2.69% |
| PAL pictures/s | 10.2829 | 10.5441 | 2.54% |

Full NTSC foreground preparation averages 182,896 cycles, down 2.85% from
188,260. Header work falls 15.07%. These are separate active-snapshot populations
on the same route, not matched-frame microbenchmarks. Whole-route rates of
19.85 NTSC / 17.56 PAL include lighter scenes; they are not the gameplay rates.
Source logic remains approximately 59.92 original steps/s on both TV families.

**This remains a diagnostic: movement is still visibly stepped.** Active NTSC
p95 gaps remain about 133 ms and the longest gap about 150 ms. No smooth 30/60 fps
claim is made. Responsive controls do not imply smooth visible animation.

## Tests requested from the human

1. Load on standard-speed MSX1, 64 KiB RAM / 16 KiB VRAM and built-in PSG.
   Select ASCII16 and 100% speed. In openMSX use an MSX1 profile, keeping the
   same machine, video and speed settings for comparison with v0.0.11.
2. Tap F1 to start. Check hat, face, torso and legs on shore and all four ice
   lanes. Expect yellow hat, cream skin/legs, gray torso; no red hat, missing
   body sections, trails or changes to sea/ice/enemy colors. Lose a life and
   restart: the death colors remain as before and the normal palette returns.
3. Move left/right and jump repeatedly with arrows or joystick. Compare with
   v0.0.11: control response and jump duration should remain the same. Report
   whether stepping improves, stays similar or worsens; a large improvement is
   not expected from the measured gain. Compare perceived speed with the Atari
   original separately from animation smoothness.
4. Watch score, temperature and lives while landing, collecting fish and dying.
   No digit should freeze, disappear or leave a previous character behind.
   Release/repress Space or joystick fire while on blue ice to reverse its row;
   confirm the usual collision, movement and sound behavior.
5. Report release, emulator/machine, TV family and speed setting. A short video
   with audio covering horizontal movement, jumps and one death is useful.
   PAL and physical MSX1 reports are welcome; keep them identified separately.
