# v0.0.6-progression — all-mode native mechanics diagnostic

This prerelease validates original scoring, reserves, alternating players,
construction/entry/bonus/progression, game over and restart in all four original
variations. Mechanics execute as native Z80; the supplied Atari ROM defines
the exact arithmetic, flags, RNG and phase order. This remains a diagnostic.

- ROM: `frostbite-0.0.6-progression.rom`, 4,194,304 bytes, **ASCII16**.
- SHA-256: `1223931972bd7e0ec02a8c66805cb30ac0defaad1abd71dd67c9c8a7746be5f6`.
- Exact tested build source: [`ac146a7`](https://github.com/sandersouza/msxgp-frostbite/commit/ac146a7c82bd5ab77a5dff2deb5d5452a0478c99).
- Standard-speed MSX1, 64 KiB RAM, 16 KiB TMS9918-family VRAM and built-in
  PSG. No expansion, turbo, disk or additional sound hardware. This build is silent.

## Automated scope and limitations

Eight new cold input-only original routes and independent full-state replays
cover all four variations (21,468 logical intervals). The current ROM matches
all 12 new/retained native routes and every one of 96 route × NTSC/PAL × layout
× width combinations: 198,560 complete CPU/all128RAM checkpoints and 182,308
completed frames checked pixel by pixel. No active canonical/contact injections
or dropped source intervals are allowed in natural routes.

Controlled checks cover 818 full progression phases, 14,828 geometry phases and
652 interaction phases. The existing source catalogs retain 50,289,350 cells and
16,776,480 physical leaf queries. All 65,536 shift/flag cases, 15 dense/cache/toggle
stress modes (3,600 frames) and 14 intentional runtime corruption controls pass.
299 original-free host tests and public source policy pass.

Named-route peaks are 49,732/59,736 cycles NTSC and 71,081/71,364 PAL. The tightest
PAL margin is **283 cycles**, on advanced one-player exhaustion/restart with
layout1/width1. Dense stress peaks at 45,789 NTSC and 64,802 PAL cycles. VRAM
spacing, VBlank commits, four-sprite occupancy and memory/device guards pass.
The margin belongs only to this exact diagnostic ROM; every HUD/art/audio change
requires fresh full-view timing acceptance.

The first candidate failed a real PAL transition budget and was rejected. The
released candidate summarizes a recognized pure source ROM-indexed write loop
under strict runtime guards; other inputs execute the original native body.
135,168 actual-Z80/source cases prove summarized and fallback behavior, complete
CPU/all128RAM, flags, untouched bytes and ABI/memory guards. No gameplay step,
collision channel or presentation workload was removed to meet timing.

Art, HUD and igloo display remain synthetic/coarse. Complete score, temperature
and reserve displays, definitive sprites, attract presentation and audio are
pending #14. Exact source state is tested automatically, so an incomplete HUD
must not be treated as a human score-display comparison. Whole-game acceptance,
manual emulator review and physical hardware evidence remain separate/pending.

## Human tests requested

1. Load the attached ROM as **ASCII16** on an ordinary 64 KiB MSX1 at normal
   speed, or an equivalent emulator. Try NTSC and PAL when available. Report
   machine/BIOS/VDP, mapper, emulator version and whether the test was on an
   emulator or physical hardware; include this release/version.
2. F1 is original Reset/start; F2 is original Game Select. Arrows or joystick
   move/jump; Space or joystick fire reverses a contacted blue row on a fresh
   press. Keyboard follows the active player; alternating players use joystick
   ports 1/2. F3 changes diagnostic layout and F4 width once per press.
3. From a cold start, use zero/one/two/three short F2 presses for regular
   one-player/regular alternating/advanced one-player/advanced alternating,
   then press/release F1. Expect the regular/advanced starting situation and
   original player switching after reserve loss in alternating modes. Test each
   joystick port on its own turn. Record selected variation and F3/F4 state.
4. Complete construction, land at the igloo entrance, wait through the bonus
   and resume on the next initialized level. Expect a complete transition,
   responsive controls afterward and no stuck bonus/reinitialization. Repeat
   with F3/F4 combinations, especially PAL layout1/width1. The coarse diagnostic
   igloo and incomplete HUD are expected; send video plus reproducible inputs
   for missing transitions, pacing changes or state loss.
5. In an alternating variation, lose reserves on both players. Expect control
   to switch according to the original remaining records, eventual terminal
   state when both are exhausted, and a fresh playable game after F1/recovery.
   Repeat a one-player exhaustion/reset. Report any wrong joystick ownership,
   early terminal state, failed restart or corrupted presentation.
6. Collect fish, contact threats while grounded/airborne, and use the left bear
   refuge during normal play. Expect the prior diagnostic's original contact
   behavior, with stable video while building, dying, restarting or changing
   diagnostic layout/width. Silence is expected. Send actual observations,
   video and emulator/physical differences; a successful boot is only a boot test.
