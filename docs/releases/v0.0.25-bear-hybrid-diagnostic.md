## Tests expected from the human

1. Open WebMSX, or load ASCII16 on a standard-speed MSX1 at100%,64KiB RAM, PSG
   only. Tap/click for sound. F1 starts/resets, arrows move/jump, Space/fire acts.
   Mobile virtual controls remain untested here.
2. Compare v24/v25 through all4 ice rows. Jump immediately after landing, steer
   both ways and reverse blue rows. Expect the same contacts, player colors and
   response; small hitches still remain.
3. Progress to the bear/day/night if practical. Watch the bear beside the player
   and both screen edges. Expect its complete silhouette/color, no seams, stale
   tiles, missing body parts or flicker. Approach shore and enter/complete igloos.
4. Check reset, death, pause/resume, temperature/score updates and sound. PAL
   users should compare carefully: regular mean slightly regresses in this build.
5. Return a short video with sound, version, NTSC/PAL, emulator/MSX and the exact
   action/level where a hitch or graphic defect occurs. Testing does not block
   the next measured optimization.
